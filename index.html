✳ HOW TO PLAY

Hands on. Music out.

Allow camera access, then try one of the three modes:

  1. Laser Harp: move either index or middle fingertip sideways across the light beams to trigger notes.
  2. Air Keys: lower a fingertip into a glowing key along the bottom of the stage. Lift it out, then tap again.
  3. Theremin: pinch your thumb and index finger together to hold a note. Move your right hand horizontally to change pitch, vertically for volume. Your left hand controls the filter.

You can always play the 8 keyboard buttons or press 1–8, even without camera access. Record captures generated audio only, not video.
